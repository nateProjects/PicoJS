picoJS 0.4.9 — pal() / palt()

X cycles: default → pal(9,12) → palt(10,true) → both combined → default. Uses tests/booty-sprites.png, sprite 35 (colours 0, 9, 10 only).

What to verify

Default sprite transparency

  • Striped background shows through the sprites' black (colour 0) background — sprites are no longer opaque rectangles

pal(9, 12) — draw-time recolour

  • Sprite row's orange pixels turn blue
  • The rectfill() swatch (colour 9) turns blue at the exact same time — pal() remaps primitives identically to sprites, proving it's one shared mechanism

palt(10, true) — extra transparency

  • Sprite's yellow pixels also become see-through (stripes show through them too), alongside the still-transparent black

Reset

  • Cycling back to "default" removes both — orange sprite pixels and the rectfill swatch return to their original orange
