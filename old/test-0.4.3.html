picoJS 0.4.3 — btnp, tables, math quirks

↑ ↓ / ← → navigate menu · Z burst sparkles at the highlighted row. No spritesheet needed — everything is drawn with primitives.

What to verify

btnp (Pico-8 auto-repeat)

  • Tapping an arrow moves the menu one row per press
  • Holding an arrow pauses for ~15 frames (¼ sec at 60fps) then auto-repeats every 4 frames
  • Releasing resets instantly — next tap moves one row again
  • Z fires exactly once per press (auto-repeat fires bursts while held)

Table helpers (add / del / foreach / all / count)

  • Z adds 12 sparkles; debug panel shows count climb
  • Sparkles expire and count drops — del() during for v of all() iteration is safe
  • No particle freezes or skips its update (would indicate iteration-during-mutation bug)

Math quirks

  • Green dot orbits the centre — driven by cos(t) / sin(t) on a 0..1 turn
  • Orbit direction: starts east (cos=1, sin=0), moves clockwise to south (t=0.25 → sin=-1 in Pico-8 convention, rendered downward because y grows down)
  • Orange arrow tracks the marker via atan2(dx, dy) — always points at it regardless of orbit position
  • Sparkle positions use rnd(), flr(), and rnd([colours]) — colours from the Pico-8 palette

Debug readouts

  • Menu label matches highlight row
  • sin / cos values change smoothly each frame
  • Burst log lines show total= after each press
