What to verify

sget() plumbing

  • Debug panel shows sget ok 128x128 once the spritesheet loads
  • sget(0,0) and sget(4,4) readouts differ — sprite 0's corner is transparent (reads 0), its centre is opaque (reads a real Pico-8 colour index)

bbox vs. pixel collision

  • Nudge the player sprite until the yellow bounding-box outline appears (bbox OVERLAP) but pixel still reads clear — the two sprites' 8x8 boxes touch in a transparent corner
  • Move further in and pixel flips to HIT once opaque pixels actually overlap — the background flashes to confirm it
  • This is the same technique (sget + rect-scan) as PicoDiver's pixel_collide(), just simplified to two same-size, non-flipped sprites
